# Consumer Price Index & Inflation Analysis

## The Business Question

Consumer prices can change differently across states, sectors, and commodity groups. This project focused on understanding **how CPI and inflation changed over time and how they varied across states, sectors, and commodities**.

Using a Consumer Price Index dataset, I analyzed the data in Excel and created an interactive dashboard to explore inflation patterns and compare price levels across different categories.

---

## Starting With the Bigger Picture

I began by looking at the overall CPI and inflation figures.

The dashboard showed an **average inflation of 4.72** and an **average CPI of 182.39**. The **highest inflation value displayed was 136.21**, with **Haryana** shown as the highest-inflation state and **Nagaland** as the lowest-inflation state.

These figures provided an overall view, but I wanted to understand how inflation changed over time and what differences existed between states, sectors, and commodity groups.

---

## How Did Inflation Change Over Time?

The yearly trend showed a clear decline in inflation between **2022 and 2024**, followed by a small increase in **2025**.

Inflation was highest at the beginning of the period, declined through 2024, and then moved upward again in 2025.

This shows that inflation was not constant throughout the period and that changes over time are important when looking at overall price trends.

---

## How Did CPI Differ Across States?

The state-wise CPI map showed differences in CPI levels across India.

The dashboard identified **Haryana as the highest-inflation state** and **Nagaland as the lowest-inflation state** based on the selected data.

This comparison shows that price levels were not uniform across states. Looking at state-level differences can help understand how inflation and consumer prices vary geographically.

---

## Looking at Different Sectors

I also compared inflation across the three sectors available in the dataset:

* **Combined**
* **Rural**
* **Urban**

The sector-wise chart showed differences between these groups, allowing the overall inflation figure to be viewed from both rural and urban perspectives.

This helps provide a broader understanding of how changes in consumer prices can differ depending on the sector.

---

## Which Commodity Groups Had Higher CPI?

The commodity-level CPI analysis showed that **Meat and Fish** had the highest CPI among the commodity groups displayed, followed by **Spices** and **Pan, Tobacco and Intoxicants**.

Other groups such as **Prepared Meals** and **Fuel and Light** showed comparatively lower CPI values within the highlighted categories.

This indicates that price levels varied considerably between different types of consumer goods.

---

## Which Commodities Had Higher Inflation?

The inflation analysis by commodity group showed that **Spices** had the highest inflation among the categories displayed.

It was followed by **Personal Care and Effects**, **Consumer Food Price Index**, **Footwear**, and **Food and Beverages**.

This highlights that inflation was not affecting every commodity group in the same way. Certain categories experienced higher price increases than others.

---

## What Did the Analysis Reveal?

The analysis showed three main patterns.

First, inflation **declined between 2022 and 2024 before increasing slightly in 2025**, showing that inflation changed over time rather than remaining constant.

Second, CPI and inflation **varied across states**, with Haryana and Nagaland appearing at opposite ends of the state comparison in the dashboard.

Finally, different commodity groups showed different price patterns. **Meat and Fish had the highest CPI among the highlighted groups**, while **Spices had the highest inflation** in the commodity-level inflation chart.

Overall, the dashboard shows that consumer price changes can look very different depending on **time, location, sector, and commodity group**.

---

## What Could This Analysis Be Used For?

This type of analysis can help identify **which states and commodity groups are experiencing higher price levels or inflation**.

For example, categories showing higher inflation could be monitored over time to see whether the increase is temporary or continues for several periods. State-level comparisons can also help identify areas where consumer prices are changing differently from the overall trend.

Further analysis using factors such as **income, food prices, fuel prices, and regional economic conditions** could help explain why these differences occur.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis showed that inflation and CPI changed over time and varied across states, sectors, and commodity groups.

The average inflation in the dashboard was **4.72**, while the average CPI was **182.39**. Inflation declined through **2024 before increasing in 2025**, while commodity-level analysis showed that **Spices had the highest inflation among the displayed categories**.

The key takeaway is that **overall inflation does not tell the complete story**. Breaking the data down by year, state, sector, and commodity group provides a clearer picture of where consumer prices are changing and which categories require closer attention.
